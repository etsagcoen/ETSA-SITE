ABOUT US

Built by students, for students

ETSA is the student body of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering at Government College of Engineering, Nagpur. It organizes academic and extracurricular activities, promotes students’ personal and professional growth, and fosters leadership and community engagement. ETSA also conducts Horizon, its annual technical extravaganza.

OUR MISSION

What we're here to do

ETSA brings together students of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering to explore beyond the classroom — through technical talks, competitions, workshops, and industry interactions. We exist to make learning collaborative and give every student a platform to build, present, and grow.

Since 2023

Running consistently for 3 years, ETSA has grown from a small student circle to a full department community.

Student-run

Every event, from planning to execution, is organised by ETC students with faculty as guiding mentors.

Open to all years

First year through final year — every ETC student is part of the ETSA community, no invite needed.
