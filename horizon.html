COMPETITIONS

What's on at Horizon 2026

Scroll down to explore every competition — from idea to prototype to signal.

01
POSTER IDEATHON
COMPETITION 01

Ideathon

Pitch a bold, workable idea to a live panel of judges. Teams get a fixed time slot to present a problem, their solution, and why it deserves to exist — no code required, just clear thinking and a sharp pitch.

  • Format Team of 2–4
  • Duration 90 sec pitch + Q&A
  • Judged on Originality, feasibility, delivery
02
POSTER IoT
COMPETITION 02

IoT Challenge

Build a working sensor-to-cloud demo on the spot. Boards, sensors, and a stable Wi-Fi network are provided — you bring the logic. Best working prototype with the cleanest dashboard wins.

  • Format Team of 2–3
  • Duration 3 hour build window
  • Judged on Functionality, dashboard, demo
03
POSTER 5G
COMPETITION 03

5G Tech Event

A technical showdown on next-gen wireless — quiz rounds, case-study breakdowns, and rapid-fire questions on 5G architecture, spectrum, and what's coming after it. For students who actually read the RFCs.

  • Format Solo or pairs
  • Duration 3 rounds, 45 min each
  • Judged on Accuracy, speed, depth
